## Added data `[Added]`
- **Geography:** the real Whistler valley. Alpine Meadows Substation sits at 50.1372, −122.9712, with Nesters substation beside it. The Pine Ridge corridor runs down the west slopes, and the pad-mounts form a grid through Alpine Meadows. Distribution feeders are drawn as context lines (not assets).
- **Zones:**
  - floodplains: River of Golden Dreams, and Fitzsimmons Creek by Nesters;
  - habitat: Green Lake wetlands, and the old-growth Pine Ridge slopes;
  - oil sites: both substations;
  - load growth: the Alpine Meadows homes;
  - people: Whistler Village and Alpine Meadows.
- **Lens exposure** = the zone value where the asset stands × a sensitivity for its kind. See `KINDS` in `whistler-additions.js`.
- **Tolerance and failure cost per kind:**

  | Kind | Tolerance | Failure cost |
  |---|---|---|
  | bank | 45 | $2.5M |
  | padmount | 35 | $60K |
  | line | 40 | $600K |
  | breaker | 40 | $350K |

- **Customers:**
  - station: ~6,200;
  - each bank: 1,550;
  - each pad-mount: 30 + 12 × its real reliability risk;
  - each Nesters breaker: 1,100.
- **Lens data sources (Canadian):** RMOW floodplain maps 2024, ClimateData.ca SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5, BC Conservation Data Centre, SARA critical habitat, CER Energy Futures 2026.
- **Investments:**
  - Catalyst stage badge mapped from the Whistler stage (Initial → Explore, Long Term Planning → Plan, Short Term Planning → Build, Executing → Results);
  - **BCR** = value per $M of spend ÷ 2,000, because Whistler "value" is a framework score, not money;
  - one-line descriptions for the 3 pad-mount alternatives and for the other real alternative names (Refurbish / Replace / Upgrade Transformer, the CCS options, Automation's two) plus Cooper's two suggestions (`ALT_DESC_MORE`);
  - **alternatives for all 45 investments** are now generated (111 real; detail and risk profiles where the source has them), and Cooper's suggested ones fill each up to three (`altsOf`, 26 `[Added]`);
  - enterprise goals (Figma copy);
  - value-measure → Strategic relevance category mapping.

## Using more of the dataset
- Pull more records with `python3 ~/.claude/skills/copperleaf-demo-data/scripts/demo_data.py <view>`, add their IDs to `scripts/make-whistler-data.py`, then re-run it. Don't paste raw JSON into prototypes.
- The 296 optimization-scenario investments (WG23…) are a separate set. Don't mix them with the 45 curated ones on one screen without saying so.
