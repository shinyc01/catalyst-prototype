# Investment logic (Build)

## From study to idea `[Figma]` + `[Confirmed]`
- From a study, "Create idea" opens a scripted Cooper chat with three questions:
  1. **Intent:** replace / harden worst first / defer with battery.
  2. **Horizon / spend limit.**
  3. **Trade-off** if the budget gets tight.
- Cooper then drafts the idea. The study's lenses and results carry over into Build: the need text, the affected assets and the "why this matters" text.

## Alternatives `[Figma]` names and figures, `[Assumed]` selection rule
| Alternative | BCR | Cost range | Timing | Potential value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Targeted Hardening | 2.5 | $2.4M–$3.8M | ~8 months | $6M–$9M |
| Full Replacement | 2.1 | $4.2M–$7.4M | 3–5 years | $8M–$11M |
| Reactive Repair | 0.9 | $0.6M–$2.2M | 2–3 weeks | $1M–$2M |

- **Which one is recommended** follows the first answer to Cooper: "harden / worst" → Targeted Hardening; "defer / battery" → Reactive Repair; otherwise → Full Replacement. `[Assumed]`
- **Other options** are the two that aren't recommended.

## Compare to other investments `[Figma]` layout, `[Assumed]` maths
- **The portfolio** is 34 illustrative investments plus this idea, giving 35.
- **Rate** = 1 + the number of investments with a higher metric. **Percentile** = (1 − (rank−1)/35) × 100. **Standing** = the quartile.
- **Investment position** is a histogram of the portfolio by BCR ("Value vs Cost") or by risk reduced per $M ("Efficiency"). The bin holding this idea is highlighted.
- **Similar investments** are the 2–3 neighbours above and below this idea in that ranking.
